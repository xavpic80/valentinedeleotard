(01) — In a nutshell

Two decades building brands, campaigns and teams across Canada and Europe. From packaging and product to campaigns, digital and brand identity, I make work that makes people stop scrolling, smile, or suddenly feel hungry.

Parisian-born and Parisian-raised, which basically means I have opinions about bread and typography. I began my career in advertising at M&C Saatchi, moved to Canada in 2010, and spent the next years shaping brands like DAVIDsTEA and Goodfood, building creative studios from the ground up and leading teams of 25+ creatives.

And yes, I take creativity seriously. Myself? Not always.
